Who we are.

Identity

Philippine Youth for Christ (PYC) is a youth-initiated and youth-led supporting ministry within the Seventh-day Adventist Church, working alongside Adventist Youth Ministries to lead young people to Christ and equip them for evangelism.

Vision

A generation of youth upholding the distinctive message of the Seventh-day Adventist Church, excelling in every part of life, and embracing radical discipleship and a life of missionary service.

Mission

To galvanize young people toward radical commitment to Christ, and to mobilize youth and young adults for the message and mission of the Adventist Church and the proclamation of the Three Angels’ Messages.
