WHO WE ARE

IDENTITY

Philippine Youth for Christ (PYC) is a youth-initiated and youth-led supporting ministry within the Seventh-day Adventist Church, committed to working closely with Adventist Youth Ministries in leading young people to Christ, nurturing, and equipping them for evangelism.

VISION

PYC envisions a generation of youth upholding the distinctive message of the Seventh-day Adventist Church; aspiring to excel in all aspects of their lives — academic, professional, and spiritual; embracing the call to radical discipleship and a life of missionary service.

MISSION

PYC seeks to galvanize young people towards radical commitment to Christ and to mobilize youth and young adults to be fully committed to the distinctive message and mission of the Adventist church towards the proclamation of the Three Angels' Messages.

SPIRIT OF PYC

The Spirit of PYC describes the core guiding principles of this movement. All PYC participants are expected to uphold these values:

A respect for Scripture · An appreciation for the Spirit of Prophecy · A quest for Biblical holiness · A vibrant worship experience · A passion for lost souls · A cultivation of godly relationships · An exemplary and abundant lifestyle · An enthusiasm for service · A commitment to the Seventh-day Adventist Church · An attitude of humility and cordiality
