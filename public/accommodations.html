How does the roommate request work? ▾

1. You can request up to 3 people to room with (max 4 per room including yourself).

2. All roommates must be the same gender (boys with boys, girls with girls).

3. The people you request must already be registered for PYC 2026.

4. Each person can only be part of one roommate group. If someone has already been requested or made their own request, they cannot be added to yours.

5. Roommate requests are not guaranteed. The PYC Accommodations Team will do their best to honor requests, but final room assignments are subject to availability.

6. You do not need to request roommates to reserve a dorm. If you don't make a request, you'll be assigned roommates by the team.

Terms & Conditions

1. Accommodations Occupancy
Room / tent occupancy limits are strictly enforced. Only officially registered PYC participants who have booked an accommodation slot are permitted to occupy assigned rooms / tent areas.

2. Room Assignment
Room assignments / tent areas will be managed by the PYC Accommodations Team. Special requests (e.g., roommate requests) are subject to availability and are not guaranteed.

3. Cancellation & Transfers
Accommodation reservations are non-refundable but transferable. Transfers are only allowed within the same accommodation type and gender.

4. Room Care & Damages
Participants are responsible for maintaining cleanliness and order in their assigned rooms / area.

5. House Rules Compliance
All occupants are expected to comply with accommodation policies and guidelines at all times.

6. Security & Liability
The organizers are not liable for any loss, theft, or damage to personal belongings. Participants are advised to secure their valuables at all times.

7. Changes & Adjustments
The PYC Accommodations Team reserves the right to make necessary adjustments to room assignments or arrangements due to unforeseen circumstances. Participants will be duly notified of any such changes.
