Terms and Conditions

Completing the registration process and submitting payment constitutes an agreement on your part to accept the following terms and conditions:

Registration Fee

  1. You agree to pay in full the fee that is applicable to your registration category.
    Note: Registration fee is refundable and transferable until the end of registration period ONLY. Please contact us via FB page for processing the refund.

Travel

  1. You are responsible for securing your own travel and making payment for such. Cancellation of travel expenses incurred are the sole responsibility of each individual.

Minor Participation

  1. For minors, you must secure an Assumption of Risk, Release and Waiver of Liability for Minor Participation Form duly signed by your parent or guardian. The waiver shall be given to the organizers upon onsite registration.

Medical Health

  1. For those with medical condition that requires special attention, you must inform the organizers in writing so as to facilitate necessary actions. Parents/guardians must sign a form of authority in the event of emergency hospital treatment. The organizers will administer minor first aid only when necessary.

Disclaimer

  1. The organizers shall use the personal information of attendees for conference purposes only.
  2. The organizers reserve the right to alter any schedules, speakers, and program content should unforeseen circumstances require it. The organizers are not responsible for resulting costs or inconvenience to participants in this case.

Refund & Transfer Policy

Philippine Youth for Christ

To ensure smooth preparation and allocation of resources for our events, please take note of the following policy:

1. Strictly No Refund Policy

All registration fees are non-refundable once payment has been confirmed. This policy applies regardless of personal schedule conflicts, emergencies, or failure to attend the event.

2. Transfer of Registration

Registration may be transferred to another delegate. The original registrant is responsible for:

  • Finding a replacement delegate
  • Informing the organizing team within the specified deadline
  • Providing complete details of the replacement delegate

3. Shirt Size Policy

Once registration is confirmed, shirt sizes can no longer be changed. The replacement delegate will receive the originally registered shirt size.

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation as this policy allows us to prepare efficiently and steward resources responsibly for the success of our event.

For transfer requests and concerns, please contact the event organizing team within the given timeframe.
