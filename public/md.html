Fix Missing PYC Numbers

Assigns confirmation numbers to Paid / Pending Review registrations that are missing one (caused by the old numbering bug), then emails each person a correction notice with their new number. Leave the boxes empty to fix everyone missing a number, or paste a specific list to limit it. Always Preview first.

If unchecked, Pending Review people get a number + correction email but their status stays Pending Review (you approve their proof later). If checked, they are marked Paid now.
